OrigoNex Learn is a private study app for Windows, made for one family. It is not a public service and is not sold.

It helps a Year 7 pupil revise with notes, flashcards and quizzes for her school subjects, with an AI tutor that gives hints instead of answers, safety filtering, a parent report, and her own Google Calendar for tests and homework.
