OrigoNex Learn privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What OrigoNex Learn is

OrigoNex Learn is a private study app that runs on one family's own Windows computer. It is used only by members of that household and is not offered to the public.

Google data

When a Google account is connected, OrigoNex Learn can read events in the chosen Google Calendar and add events the user confirms, such as tests, homework and revision sessions. It does this only to show the user their own calendar and to carry out actions they ask for. It does not read Gmail or any other Google data.

Google data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the family's own computer. They are not sent to the app's author or to any server run by the app's author, and they are not sold or shared for advertising.

When the user asks a question or makes study material, the relevant text may be sent to Claude (Anthropic) through the family's own Claude account so it can help. That use is covered by the account holder's agreement with Anthropic.

OrigoNex Learn's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Activity log for parents

The app keeps a log of study activity and safety checks on the computer so a parent can review it. Parents can delete it at any time in the app's parent area.

Removing access

Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling OrigoNex Learn and deleting its folder in AppData removes all data it stored.

Contact

Use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for OrigoNex Learn.
